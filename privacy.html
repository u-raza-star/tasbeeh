Privacy Policy — Digital Tasbeeh

Last updated: 18 November 2026

Digital Tasbeeh ("the app") is a dhikr counter for Android. This policy explains what data the app handles and where it goes. In short: your data stays on your phone and, if you choose to sign in, in your own Google Drive. The developer does not collect, receive or sell your data.

Data stored on your device

Your tasbeehs, counts, targets and app settings are stored locally on your phone so the app works offline. This data never leaves your device unless you turn on Google Drive sync.

Google sign-in and Drive sync (optional)

  • If you tap "Continue with Google", the app asks Google for your email address (to show which account is connected) and for access to the app's own private folder in your Google Drive (the "app data" folder, scope: drive.appdata).
  • The app can only see and manage its own app-data folder. It cannot see, read or change any other files in your Drive.
  • When sync is on, your tasbeehs, counts and settings are copied to that private folder so you can restore them after reinstalling the app or on a new phone.
  • Your data is stored in your Google account. The developer has no access to it.

What the developer collects

Nothing. The app has no analytics, no advertising, no tracking and no server of its own. No personal data is sent to the developer or to any third party.

Deleting your data

  • Sign out in the app (Settings → Account & Sync) to disconnect your Google account.
  • Uninstalling the app removes the data stored on the phone.
  • To remove the Drive backup, open Google Drive → Settings → Manage apps → Digital Tasbeeh → Delete hidden app data, or disconnect the app from your Google Account permissions page.

Children

The app is a general-purpose counting tool and does not knowingly collect data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
