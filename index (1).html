Digital Tasbeeh

A simple, beautiful dhikr counter for Android.

Digital Tasbeeh helps you keep count of your daily dhikr and tasbeeh with a large, easy tap button, custom targets, voice counting and tap feedback. You can create as many tasbeeh entries as you like and pick up exactly where you left off.

Google Drive backup

If you sign in with Google, the app can back up your tasbeehs, counts and settings to your own Google Drive (in the app's private app-data folder) and restore them when you reinstall the app or move to a new phone. Sync is optional: the app works fully offline, and signing in is never required to use the counter.

Features

  • Large tap counter with optional target and progress ring
  • Multiple tasbeeh entries, saved with their counts
  • Voice counting, vibration and click feedback
  • Dark and light themes, English and Urdu interface
  • Optional Google Drive backup and restore (app-data folder only)
